# leseli
Leseli is a web platform built for Basotho students planning life after grade 11. Enter your LGCSE results and instantly discover every university programme you are eligible for across Lesotho's institutions, with AI-powered guidance to help you choose the right path.

# The problem

Most students in Lesotho finish Form E without knowing which university programmes their LGCSE grades actually qualify them for. They rely on word of mouth, guess, or miss opportunities entirely. There is no single place to check eligibility, compare institutions, read prospectuses, and get personalised guidance until now.

# What Leseli does
Eligibility matching — enter your LGCSE subject grades and get an instant list of every programme you qualify for across Lesotho's universities
Smart recommendations — AI ranks your eligible courses by best fit based on your grades, interests, and goals
AI course explainer — click any course and get a plain-language explanation of what it involves, career paths, and whether it suits your profile
AI advisor chat — have a conversation with an AI counsellor that asks you questions and helps you decide what to study
Scholarships — discover funding opportunities relevant to your results and chosen field
Prospectuses — access institution prospectuses in one place without hunting across multiple websites
Institution comparison — compare programmes side by side across NUL, LIMKOKWING, Botho, and other institutions
